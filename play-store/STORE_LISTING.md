BERUR TRADES by VinTech brings market-monitoring tools and technical analysis into one mobile workspace.

Explore market observations, review technical indicators, follow synthetic-market data, and study market conditions with a clean dashboard designed for mobile devices.

FEATURES
• Market dashboard with live public market observations where available.
• Technical-analysis tools and market-strength comparisons.
• Digit and volatility-market statistics for educational research.
• Forex and CFD chart-study tools, including timeframe and setup review.
• Economic-calendar information and market-event awareness.
• Local analysis history and a mobile-friendly interface.
