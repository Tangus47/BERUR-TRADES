Privacy Policy

BERUR TRADES by VinTech — Market Analysis Edition

Effective date: 5 November 2026

1. About this edition

This Google Play edition provides market charts, public market observations and educational technical-analysis tools. It does not connect to brokerage accounts, collect broker credentials, place trades, or open binary-options trading platforms.

2. Information we collect

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, financial-account details or payment information in this edition. We do not operate advertising or analytics SDKs in the Android app.

Some analysis preferences and locally generated analysis history may be stored in the app's browser storage on your device. This information is not intentionally transmitted to VinTech servers by this edition. You can clear it by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

3. Third-party services and network requests

The app loads its interface from GitHub Pages and may request public market-calendar information from ShowMyTrades and display chart content supplied by TradingView. These providers may receive standard network information such as your IP address, device/browser information and request details when your device connects to their services. Their processing is governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

  • TradingView Privacy Policy

4. Data sharing and sale

VinTech does not sell personal information. This edition does not provide VinTech with a user profile or brokerage account information. Third-party providers may process network data as described in their own policies.

5. Security and retention

Locally stored analysis information remains on your device until you clear app storage or uninstall the app. Network traffic to supported services uses HTTPS where available. No internet-connected service can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Children's privacy

This app is not designed for children and is not directed to children under 13.

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The latest version will be published at this page.
