US DOLLAR INDEX — DXY

Manual confirmation: choose the current DXY direction after reviewing the chart. No unverified price feed is assumed.

Track the US dollar against its major-currency basket and compare its movement with the selected USD pair.

TVC:DXY · LIVE CHART
DXY RISING
Often supports USD strength: EUR/USD, GBP/USD, AUD/USD and NZD/USD may face downward pressure; USD/JPY, USD/CHF and USD/CAD may receive upward pressure.
DXY FALLING
Often the reverse: USD-quoted major pairs may strengthen while several USD-base pairs may weaken. Confirm with each pair's own chart.
DXY RANGE / MIXED
Treat the dollar as lacking a clear broad direction. Avoid forcing a USD bias from a flat or conflicting index.

HOW TO USE THE WARNING

A high-impact event inside your selected before/after window is flagged as NEWS WINDOW. Treat it as a reason to review spreads, slippage, volatility and existing exposure—not as a buy or sell signal. Actual-versus-forecast differences do not automatically establish direction; compare the release with market expectations and price action.

Synthetic Volatility indices, Boom and Crash are excluded from macroeconomic news warnings.

Calendar provider: ShowMyTrades Economic Calendar · API documentation. Events may be revised, moved or incomplete. Verify important releases against the official publisher before trading.
